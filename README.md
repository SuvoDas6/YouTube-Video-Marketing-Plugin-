# YouTube-Video-Marketing-Plugin-
A WordPress YouTube Video Marketing Plugin that pauses videos at a selected time and opens a lead-generation popup form. After successful form submission, the video automatically resumes from the paused position. Ideal for lead generation, sales videos, webinars, product promotions, and conversion tracking.
# YouTube Video Marketing Plugin for WordPress

A powerful WordPress plugin for creating interactive YouTube video marketing campaigns with timed video pauses, lead-generation popup forms, and automatic video continuation after form submission.

## Features

* 🎥 Embed YouTube videos anywhere on your WordPress website
* ⏱️ Pause the video automatically at a selected time
* 📝 Display a lead-generation form in a popup when the video pauses
* ▶️ Automatically resume the video after the form is successfully submitted
* 🎯 Support custom pause times for different marketing campaigns
* 📋 Customizable form fields such as name, email, phone, and other information
* 💾 Store form submissions in WordPress
* 📧 Send email notifications for new leads
* 📊 Support marketing and conversion tracking
* 🔗 Compatible with Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, and other tracking solutions
* 📱 Responsive popup design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
* ⚙️ Easy-to-use WordPress admin settings
* 🔌 Shortcode-based implementation for flexible placement

## How It Works

1. Add a YouTube video to your WordPress page.
2. Set the desired pause time from the plugin settings.
3. The video plays normally for the visitor.
4. At the selected time, the video automatically pauses.
5. A lead-generation popup appears with the configured form.
6. The visitor submits the form.
7. After successful submission, the popup closes.
8. The video automatically continues from the exact paused position.

## Example Shortcode

```text
[youtube_marketing_video id="VIDEO_ID" pause_at="60" form="lead_form_1"]
```
